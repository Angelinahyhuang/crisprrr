Safety

Three ways it could go wrong.

  1. Natural killer cell attack

    Without HLA, natural killer cells attack the organ’s own cells.

    Fix: retain HLA-C to avoid both T-cell rejection and NK attack
  2. Off-target edits

    Long-term cancer risk from edits in oncogenes or tumor suppressors.

  3. Organ viability

    Editing takes 12 hrs; the liver must be delivered within 8–18 hrs of removal.
